## Idea sourcing

## Buy criteria (ordered roughly as he states them)
1. Rapid revenue growth. Hurdle drifted up: 20–25% (old) -> 35%+ (Jul 2018) -> 40%+ "and sometimes more" (Jan 2020 KB). SaaS names 40–70%. [PICK, KB1, VAL]
2. Special niche / moat / "rule breaker"; leader in new field or disrupter, not an also-ran; not a commodity product. [PICK, KB1, VAL]
3. Recurring revenue ("God, this is important!"). Usually SaaS; NOT selling things/hardware. Resilient in slowdowns. [PICK, KB1]
4. High gross margins ("I really want high gross margins"); not capital intensive, low capex. [KB1, VAL]
5. Rapidly improving metrics: losses as % rev falling fast, profits rising, GM rising, customer adds, cash flow improving, opex % rev falling. Tolerates S&M % not falling if greenfield land-grab AND revenue really growing. [PICK, KB1]
6. Dollar-based net retention: floor >100% (2018) -> >110% (2020 KB); looks for >120%; impressed >130%. Exited HubSpot when CFO "thought" ~100%. [PICK, KB1, VAL]
7. FCF positive and growing, or clear progress toward it. [KB1]
8. Lots of cash, little/no debt (no net debt). [PICK, KB1, VAL]
9. Founder-led, substantial insider ownership. [KB1, VAL]
10. No big customer concentration (top 3 at 30–40% = bad). [PICK, KB1]
11. Long runway: must plausibly 3–4x ("wouldn't buy at $45 unless it could get to $150"). Small share of large/growing TAM; beware law of large numbers. [KB1, VAL]
12. Management must care about profit eventually (sold AMZN for this reason). [KB1]
13. GAAP loss as % of revenue at reasonable level AND falling (exited HDP at 78% GAAP loss/rev with slowing growth). [VAL]
14. "Gut": must feel comfortable with the model (never comfortable with Wix freemium). [VAL]
15. Ethics filter: avoids companies whose model he finds exploitative (ZLTQ, HZNP, GILD pricing). [KB3]

## Exclusions
- No Chinese companies at all; generally avoids foreign/EM companies (fraud, info access). [KB1, VAL]
- Avoids mining/drilling/natural resources (boom-bust), usually restaurants (limited). [KB1]
- Board off-topic: crypto, marijuana, options, TA, value/turnaround/dividend investing, market timing. Small biotechs grandfathered but not "growth stocks". [KB1]
- No bonds, options, futures. [KB1]

## Accounting stance
- Uses adjusted/non-GAAP only; ignores GAAP; excludes SBC (argues double-count with dilution). Trusts mgmt reconciliation; "if I don't trust mgmt I shouldn't own it". [KB2, KB3]
- SaaS accounting understates economics: S&M expensed upfront vs ~1/40 of lifetime revenue recognized. Deferred revenue = float. [VAL]
- NOTE for skill: user's own frameworks (ROIC/ROIIC) conflict with ignoring SBC. Flag SBC/dilution as a separate line rather than silently adopt Saul's stance? -> decide with user.

## Valuation
- 2018: "I don't really look at P/S ratios and couldn't tell you the P/S of any of my companies." Raw P/S meaningless across GM levels; growth compresses P/S fast (60% x3 yrs = 4.1x revenue). [VAL]
- Jul 2018 [PICK]: "EV/S never enters into my consideration." Valuation shows up only as: trim when price "shot up wildly"/"way out of line"; sell high-flyers whose growth doesn't warrant valuation when raising cash. [KB1, KB2]
- "COMPANY may do well but STOCK may do poorly if too much growth already priced in." [KB1]
- Legacy (profitable cos): 1YPEG = PE / trailing-12m EPS growth — his own metric; used as a screen only. [KB3]
- Will never sell totally out of a happy position on valuation alone. [KB2]

## Process / monitoring
- Read every quarterly press release + call transcript (+ investor presentation). [KB1, KB3]
- Build tables: 8+ quarters revenue, adjusted EPS, FCF by quarter; look at YoY and sequential, seasonality; trailing-12-month series; log-scale chart. [KB1]
- Judge on actual growth, not beat/miss vs consensus; adds on "revenue miss" selloffs if company growing ~50% and call shows nothing wrong. [KB1, KB2]
- CEO tone/answers on calls matter. [KB1]

## Portfolio construction
- 8–15 positions typical; 20 absolute max; prefers ~8 high conviction. [KB1, KB2]
- Position cap: rarely >15% (often "way too much"); trims at 12–15%; has let rare loves reach 20–21%. Bear: 10–15% max, nothing ≥20%. [KB1, KB2, SELL]
- Sizing ladder: "radar" 1–2%, small 3–4%, "full" = average size, not oversize. Starts small and lets winners grow; tapers in/out. Will go big immediately if he "falls in love". [KB1, KB2]
- ~100% invested; margin rarely 2–3% (2018 PICK said "no margin, no leverage"); 2 yrs living expenses in segregated cash outside portfolio. [PICK, KB1, KB2]
- In panics: sell stalwarts / low-beta names that fell least, rotate into fast growers that fell most -> concentrates. When main names overextended: trim, spread into stalwarts and try-outs -> more names. [KB2]
- Funding new ideas: (1) trim stable slow movers, (2) trim high-flyers whose growth doesn't justify valuation, (3) cut experimental positions. [KB2]

## Buying rules
- No fixed policy; no DCA schedule; never wait for a cheaper price; take at least a starter now. [KB1, KB2]
- Adds on the way up when thesis is working (not if pure hype). [KB2]
- Adds on market-wide drops to high-confidence names; does NOT average down a single name collapsing on bad news in a rising market ("watering the weeds"). Exception: good report, stock falls on estimates nonsense -> adds. [KB2]
- No price anchoring. [KB1, KB2]

## Selling rules
- Sells when: metrics change for the worse; thinks he made a mistake; lost confidence; new facts; story changed; "fallen out of love"; price "way out of line" (trim). [PICK, KB1, KB2]
- Never sells just because price went up. Trims "around the edges" when position too big. Trim-in-steps; will buy back trims on pullback. [PICK, KB1, KB2]
- Sell a little first while evaluating, then decide. [KB2]
- Ignore cost basis, ignore multi-bagger counts, ignore what sold stocks do afterwards. Opportunity cost framing: "where else can this 3.7% go?" [KB1, KB2]
- Taxes don't override (short-term vs long-term gap tiny vs risk). [KB2]
- Board-level rules (Bear, Saul-endorsed spirit): cut <10% growers first; sell rapid-but-unsustainable growth (DDD, GPRO, FIT); "if this position were cash, how much would you buy now?" (Saul: "insightful"). [SELL]
- Holding period in practice 6 months–3 years. [KB1, KB2]
- Observer summary (thejusticier): decisions on a couple-of-quarters horizon; any sign of weakening numbers -> dump rapidly. (MAS4R 2021): safety net is selling the moment the company under-performs or stops excelling. [SELL, VAL]

## Macro
- No market timing; stays invested; recession will come, doesn't guess when. [PICK, KB2]
- Contrarian on sentiment: "if even I'm scared, this must be the bottom". [KB1, KB2]
- Ignores insider selling absent other evidence; heavy insider buying is meaningful. [KB2]
