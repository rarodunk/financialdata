## 1. Quantitative thresholds he states or applies

**Revenue growth**
- In Feb 2022 he describes the target universe as "SaaS cloud companies growing at 70% or 80% a year now, with recurring revenue" [60157, 2022-02-07]. That is well above the ≥40% baseline in rhetoric.
- **He has re-anchored his growth bar upward.** "Six or seven years ago, I would have been elated with a company growing revenue at 24%, and here I have a company growing at 124%!!!" [81934, 2022-11-28]. The Oct version garbles the numbers ("28%… A Hundred and 28%") [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **~45–55% growth now counts as the low tier of his holdings, not a strong pass.** Cloudflare at "high 47% to mid 50%" for many quarters is described as "growing revenue slower than others but very steadily" [73198, 2022-07-30; 76897, 2022-10-01]. When Cloudflare printed 47% he called it "a disappointment after a number of quarters that were sequentially all over 50%" and trimmed it [85477, 2022-12-31].
- **Implied effective floor of about 50%, with trajectory mattering more than level.** He sold most of Crowdstrike at 52.8% growth with a 44.5% guide [85477, 2022-12-31]. He kept and added to Datadog at 61%: "does a company really have to apologize for 'only' growing revenue at 61%???" [85477, 2022-12-31]. He never states a numeric floor; the ~50% figure is inferred from these actions.
- **Basics over checklists.** "We have a company growing revenue at 75% per year. That's basics, not details… how many companies are growing at 75%?" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- **Sequential growth used as a decel test.**
  - Crowdstrike: "Sequential gain dropped from 12.4% a year ago and 9.6% a quarter ago, to 8.6%, the lowest it's ever been" [85477, 2022-12-31].
  - Datadog: "up 7.4% qoq. That was really a slowdown, coming down from 75% and 16% a year ago" [81934, 2022-11-28].
  - Snowflake: "Revenue growth was up 18% sequentially… up from 10% in the April quarter" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- **He decomposes acquired vs organic growth.**
  - Bill: "Total revenue was up 156%… organic core revenue was up 71%. I'll take it." [74267, 2022-08-27].
  - Sentinel: "annual revenue growth probably over 110%… but organic growth about 95% or 100%" [74267, 2022-08-27].

**NRR / DBNRR.** No new explicit threshold. Values he cites approvingly:
- Cloudflare 126–127% [73198, 2022-07-30; 81934, 2022-11-28].
- Datadog "over 130%" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- Bill 131%, up from 125% [74267, 2022-08-27].
- Sentinel 131%, then 137%, then 134% [73198; 76897; 85478].
- Snowflake "nosebleed levels at 171%" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- Monday: >120% total, >135% for 10+ users, >145–150% for $50k+ [81934, 2022-11-28].
- Mongo: "I don't have their NRR but Atlas NRR is probably very high (150 or so)" [73198, 2022-07-30]. That figure is his guess.

All of these sit within or above the baseline range (>110%, ideally 120–130%+). What is new is that he tracks **NRR by customer cohort** and its **direction** ("NRR was a record 131%, improved from 124% a year ago, and from 129% sequentially") [73198, 2022-07-30].

**Gross margin**
- **High gross margin is read as proof of pricing power.** "consistent gross margin around 89% (!)… shows clearly that they do not need to give major discounts to get customers" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- **He tolerates GM below ~75% when the trend is strongly improving.** Sentinel: "Gross margin was up 15 points to 68% from 53% a year ago" [73198, 2022-07-30]. He held it at 68–72% GM.

**Operating margin, FCF and profitability (more weight than in the baseline)**
- He lists adjusted operating margin, op cash flow margin, FCF margin and net profit margin for nearly every company. For example: "Op cash flow margin was 44%… Free cash flow margin was 32%… Net profit margin was 15%" (Crowdstrike) [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **New point: refusing to aim for profitability is a negative.** On Cloudflare: "still assuring us that they're not trying to be profitable and will stay around break even… which is not what I really want to hear. I reduced my position" [81934, 2022-11-28].
- He recognises that unprofitable names are out of favour but holds them anyway: "Not being profitable yet is definitely out in the current market… but I'm sticking with it" (Sentinel) [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Operating leverage is tracked.** Sentinel "Op margins were minus 57%… improved from minus 98% a year ago" [76897, 2022-10-01]. Monday: "Adj operating margin was -2% improved from -11%" [81934, 2022-11-28].

**Guidance behavior**
- **Low guidance from strong companies is treated as sandbagging.**
  - Monday: "it's basically impossible to average as low as 63% for four quarters with the first two quarters at 84% and 75%" [74267, 2022-08-27].
  - Datadog: "you shouldn't pay much attention to our ridiculously low guidance, but we are giving it to cover ourselves" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- **A guide to flat revenue, or a guide that confirms deceleration across several metrics, is a sell input.**
  - Mongo: "The guide was for revenue to remain flat in the next quarter" (quoted from Wilhelm, endorsed: "I couldn't have said it better myself") [76897, 2022-10-01].
  - Crowdstrike: "they guided to 44.5% growth next quarter" [85477, 2022-12-31].

**Billings / ARR / net new ARR**
- Zscaler exit: "billings growth came in considerably below expectations for the last two quarters, and the CFO called out billings as fortelling the future" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- Crowdstrike exit: "ARR missed company's guidance… dollar-wise it was up less than the quarter before sequentially for the first time ever" [85477, 2022-12-31].
- Sentinel hold: he noted the company guided "Q4 Net New ARR to be at least 20% greater than last quarter" and an ARR growth floor of ≥50% for FY24 (company statements). His comment: "[In the Q&A… they consider this a floor…]" [85478, 2022-12-31].

**Customer counts (new emphasis: cohort growth, yoy and sequential)**
- Monday: "customers spending over $50k per year, which are growing at 147%" [74267, 2022-08-27]. Later a negative: "Customers over $50k up 116% yoy… BUT they were up 231% a year ago and this quarter it only grew 14% sequentially" [81934, 2022-11-28].
- Cloudflare: "Paying customers were up just 3% sequentially [disappointing]"; $100k customers "only 9% sequentially (Sentinel's… up 28%, or 3x as much)" [81934, 2022-11-28]. He benchmarks one holding's cohort growth against another's.
- Snowflake: "Million-dollar customers exploded to 246, up by 40 or 19.4% sequentially… up 112%" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- Crowdstrike: "They added only 1460 new customers in the quarter, which is a decrease from last quarter, and up only 44% yoy" [85477, 2022-12-31].

**Rule of 40 / magic number.** He does not use these himself. They appear only as company quotes in his Sentinel notes ("Once again, we achieved a Rule of 60"; "Our magic number was over 1.2x") [85478, 2022-12-31].

**Dilution.** He cites it favourably for Snowflake: "Buying back employee RSUs to prevent dilution, which is already under 1% per year" [73198, 2022-07-30]. See section 10.

## 2. Metrics he computes or cites per company (the KPI set)

Format: each company gets a narrative bullet list (not a numeric table) under "COMPANY REVIEWS", prefaced by "when I discuss company results, I almost always use the adjusted values that the companies give" [73198, 2022-07-30].

**Recurring KPI set:**
1. YoY revenue growth, compared to a year ago and to the prior quarter. Example: "Grew revenue at 74%, which was up from 67% a year ago, but down fron 83% sequentially" [74267, 2022-08-27].
2. Sequential (qoq) revenue growth and **dollars of revenue added sequentially**: "Dollars of revenue added sequentially were $75 million, up from just $38 million" [74267, 2022-08-27].
3. A multi-quarter growth-rate string, used once for Cloudflare: "48, 48, 54, 50, 51, 53, 51, 54, 54, 54, and then 47" [85477, 2022-12-31].
4. Organic vs total growth (Bill, Sentinel); segment growth (Mongo Atlas "60% of revenue and growing at 82% yoy"; Sentinel cloud "20% of their business") [73198, 2022-07-30]; FX-adjusted growth (Monday "65% (68% FX-adjusted)") [81934, 2022-11-28].
5. ARR growth (Sentinel, Crowdstrike) and net new ARR direction.
6. Gross margin (adjusted, or subscription GM for Crowdstrike), with yoy and sequential change.
7. Adjusted operating income and margin.
8. Adjusted net income and EPS (trailing and quarterly). Datadog: "Trailing EPS is 81 cents, with 24 cents last quarter".
9. Operating cash flow ($ and margin) and FCF ($ and margin).
10. Cash balance (Cloudflare $1,636M, Datadog $1.8B, Sentinel $1.2B) [81934; 85478].
11. NRR, overall and by cohort.
12. Customer counts: total, plus $50k, $100k, $500k and $1M cohorts, yoy and sequential, and share of revenue ("$100k customers now make up 61% of revenue") [81934, 2022-11-28].
13. Guidance: next quarter and full year, raises, and his own read of conservatism.
14. Billings, for Zscaler only.
15. Qualitative items: product launches and innovation pace, partnerships, federal certifications (FedRAMP, IL-4), moat anecdotes, management enthusiasm.
16. **Stock-price metrics (new presentation):** % down from all-time high and % up from the "sell-off low", in a dedicated table: "DON'T MISS THIS TABLE!… how much each stock is up from its sell-off low" [74267, 2022-08-27]. In November he kept the Apr/May/Jun low as the reference point: "I'm not going to show how much each company is up from its Nov 9th low, because that would be cheating" [81934, 2022-11-28].
17. YTD price change per stock from the start of year or from his initial buy price [73198, 2022-07-30].

**Portfolio-level metrics:**
- Monthly YTD table, later given as "% of what I started with".
- Cumulative return since 2017 vs the S&P 500.
- Five indexes YTD: S&P 500, Russell 2000, IJS, Dow, Nasdaq.
- Position sizes "bunched by size groups".

**Not present anywhere in this chunk:** EV/S, P/S, P/E or any multiple table. No Rule-of-40 computation by Saul. No RPO figures, only a passing "RPO" mention for Snowflake.

## 3. Buy triggers / reasons for initiating or adding

- **Tiny "try-out" positions to learn a company.** "I do sometimes take a tiny position in a company to put it on my radar… If I decide to keep it, I add to my position" [73198, 2022-07-30].
  - Dec 2022: "a tiny try-out 1.5% positions in ENPH (solar energy) and in TMDX (medical transplant technology), but then closed out ENPH" [85475, 2022-12-31]. Both are non-SaaS (see section 10).
- **Adding at what he calls "ridiculously low" prices after good results.**
  - Nov: "I put the money to work adding to Bill, to Datadog, and to Sentinel (at what seemed ridiculously low prices)" [81934, 2022-11-28].
  - Datadog: "A 15% position, I added this month at what I considered ridiculously low prices" [81934, 2022-11-28].
- **A strong quarter plus profitability inflection.** Bill: "They had what I considered to be great results and I added to my position". He cited 94% revenue growth and adjusted operating income turning positive [81934, 2022-11-28].
- **The conference call can override a planned trim.** Sentinel: "It was a 13.1% position and I thought about trimming it, but after reading the conference call I added a little instead" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Rotation into "best of the best" or "safer bets".** "pulling out of, or shrinking, positions in good but lower confidence companies… and concentrating on what I see as the best of the best" [79162, 2022-10-28]. On Monday: "I could put the money in what seemed like even safer bets" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Conviction in the story.** "I am captivated by Snowflake's story and not following my own advice about position maximum size" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Restored confidence.** Crowdstrike "is back to being a high confidence position for me"; in June he added "quite a bit" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Innovation pace as a reason to prefer a name.** "I just preferred to have my money in super-innovative companies like Cloudflare, Datadog and Snowflake" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Building up the smallest positions with sale proceeds.** June: "I sold out of Zscaler and built up what had been my three smallest positions" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Re-entry with sale proceeds.** Sept: "I took a small position in Zscaler with some of the money" after the Mongo sale. No criteria were given beyond "a company which has been much discussed on the board" [76897, 2022-10-01].
- Unchanged from baseline: "I tend to keep buying as a stock price rises" [73198, 2022-07-30]. Positions are entered with "the idea of holding it indefinitely… never with a price goal" [73198, 2022-07-30].

**Generalisable sell rules from this period:**
- **Sell immediately on bad news; don't wait.** "sometimes selling when you get the news is the best thing you can do" [79162, 2022-10-28]. He repeated it with Mongo's later prices: "I could have sold in the aftermarket at $315. I did sell the next day at $269. It's now at $148" [81934, 2022-11-28].
- **Several metrics decelerating together triggers a sale even while growth is still above 50%.** Revenue, sequential growth, new customers, ARR vs guide and net new ARR all fell for Crowdstrike, and the guide confirmed it.
- **Heavy macro talk on the earnings call is a red flag.** Crowdstrike: "the overall tone was all about macro". For Mongo he endorsed FinallyFoolin's "None of my other companies talked about macro like they did".
- **Lower confidence leads to smaller size, and the first trims come from the lowest-confidence names.** Reasons include competition, not being mission-critical, an industry that struggles to reach profit, open source, or management capital allocation.
- **Usage-based revenue lowers confidence in a recession** (Datadog, Snowflake), but on its own it did not trigger a sale.
- **No price-based trims of winners.** Snowflake: "I have no intention of trimming it at present" at 20.2–20.7% [79162; 81934].

## 5. Valuation

- **No multiples (EV/S, P/S, P/E) appear anywhere in this chunk.** Valuation is discussed only qualitatively.
- **Qualitative valuation tags per company:**
  - Cloudflare has a "high valuation" [73198, 2022-07-30].
  - Monday: "They have a lower valuation than our other companies, but they do have lots of competitors" [73198, 2022-07-30].
  - Snowflake: "Share price and valuation are both way down from their heights… down to about where Warren Buffet bought it at the IPO, and it has MUCH more revenue, RPO, FCF" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Crowded conviction leads to overvaluation; this is the basis for the position cap.** "when I have been super confident in a stock, everyone else has been too, and the stock gets bid up too high" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Retrospective admission of overvaluation.** "It's evident now that our companies were probably overvalued a year ago… as the Fool says, the best companies are always overvalued" [85475, 2022-12-31].
- **Oversold / undervalued claims used to justify holding and adding.**
  - "our companies seem as undervalued at present as they were overvalued before" [85475, 2022-12-31].
  - "on average, they are at about a quarter of the stock price… with revenue on average probably about 60% to 70% higher" [85475, 2022-12-31]. The 60–70% revenue figure is his estimate, not computed.
  - "makes our companies seem way oversold to me" (repeated monthly) [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Expectations-based reasoning.** "our companies have taken to rising 20% the day after earnings… weak results have been expected and priced in" [74267, 2022-08-27]. On Monday: "the market's expectations are very low, and if they beat them significantly I'd expect a strong response" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **Change vs baseline:** he still does not use multiples. But he now explicitly accepts that valuation risk exists, admitting the overvaluation in hindsight, and he uses "ridiculously low prices" as an adding rationale.

## 7. Admissions of mistakes and lessons

- **Mongo exit delay.** "I wish I had sold in the aftermarket the day before as I could have sold at about $315… I was going out to eat… Expensive mistake!" [79162, 2022-10-28]. Lesson: "sometimes selling when you get the news is the best thing you can do" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Oversized positions underperform.** Using Datadog: "it shows once again why I no longer keep any 30% or 35% positions… Whenever I have had such a large position, it has done worse than my portfolio as a whole" [74267, 2022-08-27].
- **Breaking his own cap.** "not following my own advice about position maximum size… Yes that's just my excuse, although it makes sense too" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Zscaler second exit.** "Probably a mistake" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **Valuation in hindsight.** "our companies were probably overvalued a year ago"; "Sure we could have realized that our stocks were overvalued" [85475, 2022-12-31]. He frames the cause mostly as "a series of once in 50 year storms" [85475, 2022-12-31].
- **The bottom call was premature.** He declared no new lows since June 16, then the portfolio hit 29.3% on Nov 9: "Well here's what I got for bragging!" [81934, 2022-11-28].
- **Monday management not vetted.** "I believe that this was complicated by the apparent euphoria they had when they were growing over 100%, and which probably was when they contracted for all these extravagances" [81934, 2022-11-28]. This is a lesson about capital discipline.
- **General humility.** "I sometimes make mistakes, even big ones!" (boilerplate) [53941, 2020-12-08; 73198, 2022-07-30]. On following him: "That is an insane method to invest!" [54480, 2021-02-08].
- **Results.** 2022 was −68.4%: "down a little more than I was at the end of 2008" [85475, 2022-12-31].

## 8. Macro statements and whether they changed his actions

- **Rates.** "anticipated bad news like interest rate hikes… I feel lots and lots of bad news is already priced in" [73198, 2022-07-30]. "crushed by the Fed's four successive (and perhaps excessive) 0.75% interest hikes" [81934, 2022-11-28]. "hit by the interest rate bugabear, as well as by spending cut backs by their customers" [85475, 2022-12-31].
- **Recession thesis: subscription SaaS is resilient.** "if there is a recession, the business of a SaaS company won't get killed to the same extent as the business of a traditional company" [73198, 2022-07-30]. "traditional company will see negative revenue growth" [79162, 2022-10-28].
- **New risk distinction: usage-based vs subscription.** Snowflake: "Usage based, so it could be hit more in a recesion than a SaaS company who gets the same subscription revenue come what may" [73198, 2022-07-30]. Datadog "is less high confidence in this recession as its revenue is largely based on usage" [76897, 2022-10-01].
- **Geopolitics / Europe.** He dismissed Monday's Europe exposure: "all of that doesn't mean that there is anything wrong with Monday, the company, or its business model" [74267, 2022-08-27]. He saw the Ukraine war as a security tailwind: "Great tailwind of worries about breaches with the Ukraine war" [73198, 2022-07-30].
- **"Once in 50 year storms."** Pandemic, supply chain, invasion, inflation, "interest rates being pushed up at an almost unprecedented rate" [85475, 2022-12-31]. He adopted the CEO line "this current macro turmoil… will pass… doesn't change our long term outlook at all" [85475, 2022-12-31].
- **Effect on actions:**
  - No move to cash and no hedging.
  - Macro did shape relative conviction. He lowered confidence in usage-based Datadog and trimmed it in September, but later added at "ridiculously low prices". He preferred "safer bets" in October.
  - Company-level macro excuses were penalised: Crowdstrike "all about macro" led to a sale, and Mongo's macro talk to an exit.
- **Self-description.** "I know literally nothing about technical analysis or economics" [85475, 2022-12-31].
