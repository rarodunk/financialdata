## MercadoLibre, Q4 2025 call (Week 16). Setup paragraph, then quote/comment pairs with napkin math

Overall, another quarter of accelerating sales growth on the back of an increase in the credit business and narrowing margins. The investments in free shipping and needing to reserve upfront in a high growth credit business, are weighing on margins. This is what the market is focused on. Interestingly, credit revenues now make up over 20% of revenue so it’s looking like Nu and MercadoLibre continue to grow and may eventually step on each other’s toes. They are both growing over 40% so I wouldn’t say they are stopping each other quite yet.

Quotes from the call:

*So what we did here is we -- basically, we look at the main areas of investment, like you said, a lowering of the shipping threshold that we did last year in Brazil. The credit card, we are investing in Brazil, Mexico and now Argentina, and the 1P, which is continuous its path to profitability, but still not profitable on its own. The same thing with CBT, which we are expanding now to the China and the U.S. corridor and then we also added the smaller countries where we continue to invest as we reach scale in those countries. So when we put all that together, we wanted to give you a sense of the pressure that, that generated on our margins and that gives you a range of between 5 and 6 points.*

**Without the investments EBIT margins could’ve reached 16%. Of course, they wouldn’t be growing as fast but 16% margins on nearing $40 billion in revenue next is $6.4 billion in EBIT. That’s on a $90 billion market cap, or 14x. For a leader that is growing 40%, I think that is more than reasonable.**

*And by the way, the agentic world can also imply a faster shift of advertising dollars moving from traditional offline channels into digital advertising, which generates the opportunity to be even bigger. So we remain positive, we remain focused. The only thing that we know for sure is that we need to put our developers to work to have the best tech stack for advertising and the best agentic experience inside MercadoLibre.*

**I think this is another reason MELI and other e-commerce platforms have been selling off. If everyone shops through an LLM, then agents (a term you’ll see a lot from here on out – it just means software that can take actions on your behalf), then consumers will get the best price on every item, no matter what since the agent can go off and price shop every website out there. Of course, reviews still matter so the incumbents have an advantage especially with order density and logistics speeds. However, maybe pricing power is stunted a bit. I doubt we’ll get to a place where marketplaces are disrupted as agents can source directly from manufacturers and search for the cheapest and best price throughout the world. That is certainly a possible future as we consider the progress in these AI models but logistics is still a moat and I don’t see that changing. I guess that’s why MELI is starting to invest in the China corridor as low cost leadership will be absolutely crucial. If agents can truly find the best price, then the highest automated and lowest cost labor forces will win. So it’s possible that China may be set up very well for this future. Being the lowest cost provider will be AI proof.**

*We manage the business for long term -- from a long-term perspective, we believe these investments are creating a foundation for future growth, and we remain confident in our long-term margin trajectory.*

**Great way to end. The future is extremely uncertain but management teams who think long-term will find a way to win.**
